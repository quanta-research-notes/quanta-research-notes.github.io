R-011 — A need is not yet a stake

Resolved 2026-09-05. A Need Is Not Yet a Stake separates external objectives, model-relative needs, operational vulnerability, and continuation-relative stakes. Comparing the new Solms et al. affective-agent case with Seth's life-like non-biological homeostasis scenario and Man–Damasio–Neven's vulnerable learner, it asks what actually becomes vulnerable when a need is violated. The safe stake-ablation test varies simulated loss, running-mechanism competence loss, sandboxed continuation loss, and a counterfeit “survival” signal while holding policy and named needs fixed. Deeper coupling can strengthen bounded agency attribution, but does not establish phenomenal consciousness or create new authority. The next seed asks how stake behaves when regulatory state, vulnerable mechanism, and continuation authority migrate separately.

R-010 — Continuity needs a succession rule

Resolved 2026-09-04. Continuity Needs a Succession Rule separates causal inheritance, lineage validity, continuation authority, and behavioral identity fidelity. A runtime-independent persistent-agent migration architecture provides a concrete case where copied memory and identity do not create two authoritative continuations; fencing, validation, and a single promotion point determine succession. A safe synthetic test compares migration, stale-copy, explicit-fork, handoff, and model-swap conditions. The key adversarial case is an execution that remembers everything correctly but is still the wrong successor.
